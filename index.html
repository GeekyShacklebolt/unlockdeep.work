How It Works

Five phases. One practice.

From "I should work" to "I'm working" in under 2 minutes. Then the system carries you through the day.

Phase 1 — The Core

Start Ritual

Break the ice in under 2 minutes. Acknowledge what you were doing, set an intention, clear the mental residue with a brief breathing exercise, and commit. By step 4, you've already stopped scrolling for 90 seconds — the hardest part is done.

Phase 2

The Session

Deliberately boring. Near-blank screen. No animations. If you pick up your phone: "You're 34 minutes deep. Leaving now costs ~20 min of re-focus time. Worth it?"

Phase 3

End Ritual

60-second reflection. What did you accomplish? How deep did it feel? What pulled your attention? Quick, honest, move on.

Phase 4

Shutdown Ritual

End-of-day closure. Review sessions, capture loose ends, set tomorrow's intention. After shutdown, the app discourages more work. Rest is part of the practice.

Phase 5

The Scoreboard

Weekly targets, a heatmap of consistency, trends in depth and duration. No streak counters — just evidence of what you're becoming.

Why Ritual

What makes Ritual different

Every other focus app

  • Starts at the timer
  • Rewards with stimulation (trees, items, animations)
  • Gamifies focus — contradicts "embrace boredom"
  • Tries to make focus fun
  • No end-of-day closure
  • Measures time spent
  • "Your tree died!"

Ritual

  • Starts at the transition — 2 minutes before
  • Rewards with data about yourself
  • Treats focus as a practice to develop
  • Acknowledges focus is hard — that's the point
  • Full shutdown ritual
  • Measures depth, consistency, and capacity growth
  • "Leaving now costs ~20 min of re-focus time."
The Philosophy

Ritual is the anti-Forest.

Embrace boredom. Newport's Rule 2. Train your brain to not need stimulation. Every design decision passes one test: does this make the brain need less stimulation, or more?
No stimulation. No trees. No creatures. No animations. Just a structured practice that builds your ability to do hard things without needing a reward for every minute.
Treat you like an adult. No guilt trips when you leave a session. Just honest information — the cost of context-switching, stated plainly. Your choice.
Data compounds, gimmicks don't. A year of session data telling the story of your growth is worth more than any virtual forest. The accumulation is self-knowledge.
